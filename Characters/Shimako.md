A kind, elderly woman who spends her days in [[Daybreak Town]] caring for her granddaughter, [[Kairi]]. She is a seasoned member of [[Vulpes]] and though she has long retired from partaking in any official profession, she possesses a deep well of wisdom from spending many years immersed in the culture of the town. Perhaps she even took up a blade against the [[Darkness]] in her younger years. She dedicates herself to Kairi as the young [[Princess of Heart|Princess]]' sole caretaker, often doting on her with plentiful baked sweets, plants, and art supplies. On occassion even goes so far as to risk her own standing by allowing Kairi to sneak out with trusted individuals like [[The Wanderer]] on occassion to offer some small semblance of freedom to her sequestered granddaughter.

An enigmatic individual who is said to have founded Daybreak Town and, as their title suggests, is the one who taught the [[Foreteller|Foretellers]], masters in their own right, all they know. Nobody that the Hawthorne guild has made connections to so far has ever seen the Master of Masters, but he seems to be generally held in high regard.
